Hi, I'm Charlie.

I'm Charlie Sexton, and Zarondo Games is my studio. By day I'm a software consultant: I design business software, advise companies and train their teams. Microsoft has named me one of its Most Valuable Professionals, and I co-host an award-winning tech YouTube channel.

I've made games before, but Ebens Puffoonery is the first one I'm letting out into the world. It's coming to Steam.

It's about the Ebens: one-eyed jelly aliens so clever that there's nothing left for them to do. So they've invented a game, purely for their own amusement. They climb into bubble suits and shoot each other with air, and every hit pumps the suit up a bit more, until it pops.
