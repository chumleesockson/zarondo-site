Deleting your data

Last updated 7 November 2026

This explains how to ask us to delete information we hold about you from Facebook, Instagram or anywhere else. It takes one email.

What we hold from Facebook and Instagram

We use a tool that connects to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account through Meta's official API. It posts our videos and reads the comments on our own posts, so we can reply, hide spam and see how our posts are doing.

If you comment on one of our posts, the tool keeps a copy of your Facebook name or Instagram username, what you wrote, when you wrote it and a link to the post. It also keeps our notes on the comment, such as whether it needs a reply, and any reply we sent.

That's all it gets. You never sign in to the tool or give it access to your account, so it can't see your profile, your friends, your messages or anything you post elsewhere. For the same reason it won't appear under "Apps and websites" in your Facebook settings, and there's nothing to remove there.

How to have it deleted

Email hello@zarondo.com with the subject "Delete my data". Tell us your Facebook name or Instagram username, and the post you commented on if you remember it, so we can find everything.

We'll delete every copy we hold of your comments, our notes on them and our replies, within 30 days, and email you to say it's done. You don't have to tell us why.

Deleting our copy doesn't remove your comment from Facebook or Instagram. You can delete it there yourself, or ask us in the same email to remove it from our post. Deleting a comment there doesn't delete our copy, so email us as well.

Everything else

The same goes for anything else we hold about you, such as comments on our other social media accounts or emails you've sent us. Email us and we'll delete it. If we have to keep something for legal or accounting reasons, such as an invoice, we'll keep only that and tell you what we kept and why. Our privacy policy explains what we collect and the rest of your rights.
