A team shooter in which nobody is harmed. They do, however, get considerably larger. Then they pop.

Quick, chaotic matches

Every hit pumps you up, until you pop and splash everyone nearby. Win by popping the other team, or by getting yourself sucked down their goal.

A special on every map

Untie the other team's tanks at the Funfair and watch them spray everywhere. Grab Ebens with a crashed saucer's ray on Roswell. Win the crop circles on Meadow to heal your team and hurt theirs.

Play with friends

Split-screen on one sofa, or online. Bots fill any empty seats.

Twelve arenas, from a seaside funfair to Mars

Your own career

Pick a club, climb three leagues and go after the cup. 24 clubs, each with its own style.
