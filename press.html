About the game

A silly team shooter where every hit pumps you up, until you pop.

Ebens Puffoonery is a silly team shooter for one-eyed jelly aliens in bubble suits. Every hit pumps you up: you get bigger, slower and easier to hit, but your shots land much harder. Health packs shrink you back down. You're at your most dangerous just before you pop.

Spring, roll and charge, or combine the moves, on maps that each have their own trick. Play on one sofa or online, or take a club up three leagues in a career.

Features

  • Every map has its own trick. Untie the other team's tanks at the Funfair, grab Ebens with a crashed saucer's ray on Roswell, snipe through the airfield's scope, or win the crop circles on Meadow.
  • Your size decides where you can go. Small Ebens squeeze through vents and tunnels. Big ones can't fit through doors, but they can climb what small ones can't.
  • Spring, roll and charge. Hop or spring high, roll away in a ball, ram your air into someone, or throw a jelly grenade. Roll into a spring to launch much further, or clip your hose onto a hurt teammate and take their air.
  • Two ways to win. In Goals, you score by getting yourself sucked down the other team's goal. In Pops, the first team to the pop target wins.
  • Your own career. Pick one of 24 clubs, climb three leagues and go after the Saucer cup.
  • Play together or alone. Split-screen for up to four on one sofa, in any mix of teams, or online. Bots fill every empty seat, from Easy to Brutal.

How it's made

Ebens Puffoonery is made by one person using AI tools for much of its art and 3D models, and for its sound and music. Nothing is generated by AI while you play.

About the developer

Zarondo Games is Charlie Sexton's studio in the UK. By day Charlie is a software consultant, a Microsoft Most Valuable Professional and the co-host of an award-winning tech YouTube channel. Charlie has made games before, but Ebens Puffoonery is the first to be released. More about Charlie
