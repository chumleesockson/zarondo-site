Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

This explains what personal information Zarondo Games collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

Zarondo Games is the trading name of Zarondo Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, company number 17508779. We decide how your information is used, which makes us its "controller" under UK data protection law. You can reach us about anything on this page at hello@zarondo.com.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or any other tracking, and it doesn't ask you for anything. It loads nothing from other companies' servers.

It's hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records the technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, for security and to keep the service running. We don't see those records. GitHub explains what it does in its privacy statement.

If you follow a link to one of our social media accounts or to another site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

When you email us

If you email us, we get your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use it to reply, and to keep a record of the conversation. Our email is provided by Google Workspace.

We keep emails for as long as the conversation is useful, and delete them after two years without contact, unless we need to keep something longer for legal or accounting reasons.

Video creators and press

We contact video creators and journalists who might like to cover our games. To do that, we look at public information: channel names, public contact details, and what kinds of games a channel covers and how its videos do. We keep a list of the people we've contacted, what we've sent them (such as keys to play the game) and any coverage that follows, so we don't contact anyone twice and can see what worked.

We do this because it's in our legitimate interests to promote our games, and we keep it to what that needs. If you'd rather we didn't contact you or keep your details, tell us and we'll remove you. We delete creators we haven't heard from after a year.

Our social media accounts

We run accounts for Zarondo Games and its games on platforms such as TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X, Bluesky, Twitch, Kick and Discord. If you comment on our posts or message us there, we see what the platform shows us: your public profile name, your comment or message, and when you sent it.

We use tools that connect to our own accounts through each platform's official API, with the platform's permission. They post our videos, and read the comments and figures on our own posts, so we can reply, remove spam and see how our posts are doing. They only work with our own accounts, and they don't collect anything about people who haven't interacted with our posts.

We use this information because it's in our legitimate interests to run our accounts and talk to our community. We keep comments and messages for as long as they stay on the platform, and our own copies for no more than two years. Each platform also has its own privacy policy, which applies to everything you do there.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone for their own use. The services we use to run the company, such as Google for email and the social media platforms themselves, handle it for us.

Some of these companies store information outside the UK, mostly in the United States. Where they do, they protect it with the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK–US data bridge or standard contract terms approved for international transfers.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, stop using it or limit how we use it. You can also object to us using it for our legitimate interests. Email hello@zarondo.com and we'll reply within a month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change how we handle personal information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
